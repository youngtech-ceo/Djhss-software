Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I add student marks?

Contact the support team or Higher official of the schools.

Can I edit marks after saving?

Yes, navigate back to the school mark entry and update as needed, then save again.

How do I promote students to the next class?

Use the "Promote" button on the student list page to move students to the next class for the new academic year.

How to contact technical support?

Step-by-Step Tutorial

  1. Login: Enter your credentials to access the system.
  2. Select school Mark Entry Choose the correct Class and Section,Exam type .
